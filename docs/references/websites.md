# Website Reference Registry

Reference websites inform craft and interaction decisions. They are not templates, content sources, or permission to reproduce layouts, copy, branding, imagery, or proprietary effects.

## Metacci

URL: <https://metacci.com/en/>

Use as inspiration for concise technology communication, alternating content rhythms, process and proof pacing, and keeping deeper explanation outside the first viewport.

## Vantra Studio

URL: <https://vantra-studio.webflow.io/>

Use as inspiration for confident cinematic scale, smooth navigation opening and closing, strong menu hierarchy, subsection disclosure, and restrained interaction feedback.

## Room & Pillar

URL: <https://www.roomandpillar.com/>

Use as inspiration for editorial whitespace, fewer and stronger content blocks, leadership presentation, and avoiding equal-weight card grids.

## Cypher Capital

URL: <https://www.cyphercapital.com/>

Use as inspiration for transparent three-part top navigation, a centered menu trigger and focused overlay, large typography, and high-end motion without excessive interface decoration.

## DeepBook V2 WIP

URL: <https://deepbook-v2-wip.webflow.io/>

Use as inspiration for large minimal technical figures, isometric system diagrams, sequential operational animation, and using fewer labels when geometry already communicates the idea.

## TGC translation rule

Every borrowed idea must be translated into TGC's own system:

- Operational Ink, Editorial Paper, Pure White, and Signal Green
- Archivo propositions and Manrope explanation
- Sharp planes, circular nodes, thin rules, and three-face isometric depth
- Section-relevant imagery and restrained operational motion
- Original structure and copy grounded in approved TGC evidence

If a reference pattern conflicts with `PRODUCT.md`, `DESIGN.md`, accessibility, content governance, or the adjacent section's meaning, do not use it.
