## Editorial notes

- Keep each public biography between 45 and 65 words.
- Always separate Core Team and Advisory Team.
- Names, roles, and group labels remain visible without interaction.
- Longer details may expand on demand, but the initial scan layer stays concise.

## Visual and motion direction

- Use one consistent headshot crop and treatment.
- On desktop, present the Core Team as a pinned horizontal scroll sequence with one member panel visible at a time. Advance through vertical page scrolling and release into the Advisory Team section after the last member.
- On desktop, present the Advisory Team as a pinned vertical sequence. Keep the active adviser's portrait, name, and role together in a stable left pane while the biography advances in the right detail pane. Release into the closing section after the last adviser.
- Keep every member in semantic document order. On mobile, short viewports, reduced motion, and when animation setup is unavailable, show both teams as complete vertical lists with visible names and details.
- Keep progress cues supplementary. Do not add autoplay or manual carousel controls. Update the advisory title only when its matching detail panel becomes active.
- Use subtle GSAP entry reveals for the remaining static content.
- The page does not require WebGL.
