# Platform Page Content

Status: Implemented baseline

## Public page copy

### 1. Hero

Headline: **High-performance gaming beyond the hardware barrier.**

> TGC is developing an AI-powered cloud gaming platform designed to bring demanding games to everyday devices across a wider range of network conditions.

Primary action: **Contact TGC**

Secondary action: **Explore the technology**

### 2. From barrier to access

Heading: **Access should not depend on what you own or where you live.**

> Modern games can require expensive hardware, substantial storage, lengthy downloads, and consistently fast connectivity. TGC moves demanding computing workloads into cloud infrastructure so supported devices can receive the game as an interactive stream.

**Before**

- Specialised local hardware
- Large downloads and updates
- Device-specific limitations

**With cloud delivery**

- More supported devices
- Reduced local processing requirements
- Faster access to the experience

### 3. Player-to-cloud journey

Heading: **From connection to gameplay.**

1. **Connect** — The player accesses TGC through a supported device.
2. **Match** — The platform identifies suitable available cloud infrastructure.
3. **Launch** — A remote instance runs the game.
4. **Optimise** — The platform processes input and video delivery using Artificial Intelligence.
5. **Play** — The experience reaches the player's device.

This sequence should be presented as one visual system rather than five separate cards.

### 4. Platform capabilities

Heading: **One platform, three layers of value.**

**Accessible delivery**  
Bring demanding gaming experiences to devices that would not normally run them locally.

**Adaptive infrastructure**  
Operate across cloud environments and account for geography, availability, and network conditions.

**Responsive experience**  
Use intelligent optimisation and responsive input handling to support interaction between player and game.

### 5. Value across the ecosystem

Heading: **Designed for more than one side of gaming.**

**Players**

- Access games on more devices
- Reduce dependence on hardware upgrades
- Avoid large local installations

**Publishers and studios**

- Reach audiences beyond traditional gaming hardware
- Reduce device-based access barriers
- Explore additional delivery models

**Infrastructure and technology partners**

- Support geographically distributed demand
- Apply compute capacity to interactive workloads
- Collaborate on delivery and performance optimisation

### 6. Technology transition

Heading: **Under the platform, every interaction becomes a delivery problem.**

> Predictive delivery, cloud orchestration, GPU utilisation, video compression, and responsive input handling work together beneath the platform experience.

Action: **Discover the technology**

### 7. Partnership close

Headline: **Bring your games, infrastructure, or market to the conversation.**

> Contact TGC to discuss platform access, publishing opportunities, infrastructure partnerships, and technology collaboration.

Primary action: **Contact TGC**

## Editorial notes

- Keep visible copy between 450 and 600 words.
- Platform owns outcomes, audiences, and the simple player-to-cloud journey.
- Do not explain AV1, UDP, CUDA, patents, or test methodology here.
- Add no FAQ until answers are approved.

## Visual and motion direction

- Use one interactive or static player-to-cloud diagram for sections 2 and 3.
- A Three.js explanation may show the workload leaving the device and reaching a cloud instance.
- Present the three platform value layers as spacious editorial text rows without repeating the homepage's isometric figure style. Use a single section image only when it adds approved, relevant evidence; reveal the three rows in reading order with a restrained stagger.
- On desktop, present the three ecosystem audiences as a pinned vertical sequence with the active audience title on the left and its benefit list on the right. Advance one audience per scroll step, then release into the technology transition.
- On mobile, in short viewports, when reduced motion is requested, or when animation setup is unavailable, show all audience subsections as a vertical stack.
- Use Lenis and subtle GSAP progression; avoid decorative particles.
- Provide a complete static fallback and reduced-motion experience.
