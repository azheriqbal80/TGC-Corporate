## Scope and mode

- Route: `/contact`
- Mode: Operate.
- Audience: prospective platform, technology, infrastructure, publishing, commercial, media, and corporate contacts.

## Visitor job and action

- Provide enough structured context for TGC to understand and route an enquiry.
- Primary task: complete the enquiry form with clear validation and preserved information.

## Content and constraints

- Use the fields and enquiry categories in `content/pages/contact.md`.
- Do not publish personal executive details, an unapproved address, or an invented general inbox.
- Do not claim successful delivery until a secure server-side endpoint and approved recipient exist.

## Surface direction

- Extend the Live Dispatch Network as a split enquiry desk: signal-green introduction beside an editorial paper form.
- The form is the page's main event; guidance follows as a single supporting register.
- Motion is limited to one entrance and never blocks form interaction.

## Current states

- Client-side required-field and email validation is implemented.
- The first invalid field receives focus and a form-level alert is announced.
- A valid test submission produces an explicit not-sent configuration notice while preserving entered information.

## Unresolved

- Secure server endpoint, role-based recipient, privacy policy, retention rules, bot protection, rate limiting, legal company name, and public address require company approval and backend implementation.
