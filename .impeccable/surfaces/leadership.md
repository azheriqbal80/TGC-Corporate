## Scope and mode

- Route: `/leadership`
- Mode: Persuade through credibility and clarity.
- Audience: prospective partners, media, candidates, and stakeholders evaluating the people behind TGC.

## Visitor job and action

- Scan every leader's name, role, group, and relevant experience without interaction.
- Understand the distinction between the operating Core Team and external Advisory Team.
- Primary action: contact TGC. Secondary action: return to the Company page.

## Content and constraints

- Use the biographies in `content/pages/leadership.md`.
- Keep Core Team and Advisory Team visibly separate.
- Do not use personal contact details, unapproved headshots, employer logos, confidential adviser information, or fundraising material.

## Surface direction

- Extend the Live Dispatch Network as an editorial people ledger rather than a conventional portrait-card grid.
- Memorable moment: consistent typographic portrait plates provide visual identity while preserving an honest no-headshot state.
- Names and roles remain visible at all breakpoints; biographies stay readable without accordions or carousels.

## Unresolved

- Names, roles, external titles, affiliations, education, experience statements, LinkedIn links, and professional headshots require final company approval before publication.
